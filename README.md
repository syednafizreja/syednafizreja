## About

I started by cycling and breaking real superelastic NiTi components at Denco Manufacturing, after a BEng in Civil Engineering at Southwest Petroleum University. The solvers came after that: a place to test where a clean structural idea meets an earthquake, a buckling imperfection, or a carbon count. The question under the repositories is simple. Does the efficient idea still hold once the inconvenient physics is allowed back in?

## Still open

- A return map with temperature, a third dimension, and a fatigue count that survives more than one earthquake.
- A shell analysis that keeps the dent. The mode-1 imperfection already cuts the thickness gain from 25.74% to 15.03%, and the comparison is still elastic.
- A lighter frame that does not hand the carbon saving back. The median whole-building drop is 5.7% on 32 of 33 frames. The NiTi devices cost 620.9 kgCO2e.

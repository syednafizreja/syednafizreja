# Syed Nafiz Reja Hasib

**Civil engineer · Computational design of low-carbon, resilient structures**

I build open, reproducible tools that link structural analysis with embodied carbon, so that low-carbon design decisions can be made early, when they matter most. I am preparing for a research master's and PhD in Europe.

---

### Current focus

- **ShellBench** *(in progress)* — How much embodied carbon do thin-shell floors save in code-designed RC frames in seismic zones, once every knock-on effect is counted? Anchored on my own bachelor thesis building and validated against my hand calculations.
- **Parametric canopy carbon optimisation** — browser tool linking thin-shell canopy geometry, simplified structural response and embodied carbon, with an evolutionary search for lower-carbon forms. → [repository](https://github.com/syednafizreja/parametric-canopy-carbon-optimization)

### Research interests

- Computational and parametric structural design, optimised for embodied carbon
- Machine-learning surrogate models for early-stage structural design
- Life-cycle assessment and durability of infrastructure materials
- Minimum-material structures for constrained environments (long-term: extraterrestrial habitats)
